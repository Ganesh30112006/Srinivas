Good morning everyone. Today we start a new topic, the process of photosynthesis.
Plants take in carbon dioxide from the air and water from the soil.
Using the energy of sunlight, the chlorophyll in their leaves turns these into glucose and oxygen.
The glucose stores energy for the plant, and the oxygen is released into the air.
Remember this for the exam. Photosynthesis happens mainly in the leaves, inside small structures called chloroplasts.
